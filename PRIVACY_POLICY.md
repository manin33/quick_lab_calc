# Privacy Policy for QuickLab Calc

Effective date: July 2026

QuickLab Calc is a laboratory calculation tool designed for routine solution-preparation calculations, including dilutions, molarity calculations, percent solutions, concentration conversions, serial dilution planning, and solution scaling.

## Information We Do Not Collect

QuickLab Calc does not require users to create an account.

QuickLab Calc does not ask for, collect, or store:

- Names
- Email addresses
- Phone numbers
- Passwords
- Payment information
- Precise location
- Contacts
- Photos
- Files
- Health information
- Personal laboratory records
- Patient information

## Calculator Inputs

QuickLab Calc uses the numerical values entered into the calculator fields only to display calculation results.

Users should not enter confidential, proprietary, regulated, patient-related, or personally identifiable information into the app.

## Technical Information

Because QuickLab Calc is hosted as an online web application, basic technical information may be processed by the hosting platform as part of normal web service operation. This may include information such as:

- IP address
- Browser type
- Device information
- Operating system
- Usage logs
- Approximate access time

This information is handled by the hosting provider as part of operating the web service.

## Data Sharing

QuickLab Calc does not sell user data.

QuickLab Calc does not share calculator inputs with advertisers, data brokers, or third-party marketing services.

## Children’s Privacy

QuickLab Calc is intended for students, laboratory workers, educators, and general users interested in laboratory calculations. It is not specifically directed toward children.

## Disclaimer

QuickLab Calc is a calculation aid for educational and laboratory planning purposes only.

Users must verify all calculations according to their laboratory SOP, analytical method, supervisor requirements, institutional policies, and applicable regulations.

The developer is not responsible for laboratory errors, misuse, incorrect inputs, incorrect interpretation of results, or failure to verify calculations before use.
